# FREN Inc.

**Spaces for care, learning and play.**

FREN is an experience design studio based in Richmond Hill, Georgia. We bring physical spaces, original artwork and interactive experiences together for the places where children and families learn, play and heal.

We design for museums, healthcare environments, education and public spaces. Our work invites people to explore, connects them with stories and makes unfamiliar places feel more welcoming.

## What we do

**Physical environment design**  
We shape spaces around the people who use them, bringing layout, materials, wayfinding and environmental graphics into one coherent experience.

**Interactive experiences**  
We create responsive environments that invite participation through movement, light, sound and play.

**Art and storytelling**  
We make original illustrations, murals and installations that reflect a place, its community and the stories it has to tell.

**Strategic design**  
We turn research and conversation into a clear direction, developing and testing ideas from the first concept through installation.

## How we work

Listening comes first. We work alongside children, families, educators, care teams and project partners to understand what a place needs and how people experience it.

Our designers, storytellers and makers bring different disciplines together around a shared idea. We test, refine and build collaboratively, paying attention to the details that make an experience feel natural and inviting.

**Listen. Include. Adapt. Grow.** These principles guide how we work with one another and with the communities we design for.

## Our work

From children's museums and therapeutic playrooms to hospital environments and community parks, our projects create opportunities for care, discovery and connection.

### Website inquiries

To enable direct submissions on GitHub Pages, set the GitHub Actions repository variable `FREN_INQUIRY_FORM_ID` to the ID of a Formspree form with `support@freninc.com` as its verified target email. For local testing, set the same variable in `.env.local`. Rebuild after changing the ID.

With an empty ID, the form opens a draft in the visitor's email app.
