About

Design that signifies strength

We are FREN, an experience design studio in Richmond Hill, Georgia. We bring physical spaces, original artwork and digital experiences together for the places where children and families learn, play and heal.

Our capabilities

From a first idea
to a place that matters.

We connect design, storytelling and technology across healthcare, education, cultural institutions and public spaces.

01

Physical environment design

  • Environmental design
  • Wayfinding
  • Materials & finishes
  • Play spaces

We shape spaces around the people who use them. Layout, materials, lighting and environmental graphics work together to make places welcoming, engaging and easier to navigate.

02

Digital innovation

  • Interactive media
  • Immersive experiences
  • Augmented reality
  • Animation

We create responsive experiences that invite participation. Original content and intuitive interactions connect the digital and physical worlds, keeping the story and the visitor at the center.

03

Installation art

  • Original illustration
  • Murals
  • Spatial storytelling
  • Large-scale installations

We bring stories into the spaces people share. From a small character detail to a large installation, we create original artwork that reflects a place, its community and the people passing through it.

04

Strategic design

  • Research
  • Experience planning
  • Concept development
  • Prototyping

We begin by listening and observing, then turn what we learn into a clear direction. Research, collaborative design and testing guide the experience from its earliest concept through installation.

Our principles

Thoughtful work begins
with how we work together.

Our values shape the conversations we have, the decisions we make and the experiences we create.

  1. 01

    Value every interaction

    Listen closely. Treat every conversation as an opportunity to understand someone better and build a stronger connection.

  2. 02

    Embrace individual difference

    Make room for different perspectives and talents. The people we work with and design for help us see possibilities we would miss alone.

  3. 03

    Demonstrate vast flexibility

    Adapt to the needs of each place and project. Bring different disciplines together to find a thoughtful way through new challenges.

  4. 04

    Constantly pursue growth

    Stay curious, test ideas and keep learning. Each project is a chance to improve our craft and discover what we can do next.

Our process

Listening comes first.

We work alongside children, families, educators and care teams to understand everyday needs and make the experience work in its setting.

  1. 01

    Deep listening

    Observe the setting and hear from the people who use it.

  2. 02

    Thoughtful research

    Understand the context, the audience and the opportunities.

  3. 03

    Collaborative design

    Develop the story and experience with our partners.

  4. 04

    Rigorous testing

    Prototype, gather feedback and refine the details.

  5. 05

    Sustained partnership

    Support the installation and keep improving together.
