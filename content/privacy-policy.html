01 / Who we are

FREN Inc. is a design studio in Richmond Hill, Georgia. We create brands, environments, original content and interactive experiences.

This policy covers our website, inquiries and business relationships with clients, prospective clients, suppliers and collaborators.

When we process personal information on a client's behalf, the relevant project agreement and data protection terms also apply.

Our work for museums, hospitals and other organizations does not make their websites, applications or visitor records part of this website.

02 / Information we receive

  • Contact details. Your name, email address, organization and other details you choose to provide when you contact us.
  • Project information. Briefs, goals, schedules, budgets, reference materials, attachments and correspondence you share when discussing work.
  • Business records. Billing contacts, invoice details, contracts, approvals and other records needed to manage a professional relationship.
  • Technical information. Website requests send information such as your IP address, requested page and browser details to the hosting service. The service can record this information for delivery, security and troubleshooting.

You can provide information through correspondence, meetings, project documents or website features where available. Please share only information relevant to your inquiry or project.

03 / How we use information

We use information to respond to inquiries, assess requirements, prepare proposals and deliver design services. We also coordinate feedback, manage invoices and maintain business records.

Other purposes include protecting our services, resolving disputes and meeting legal obligations. Technical information supports website delivery, security and improvement.

Where we offer newsletters or studio updates, we send them according to your preferences and applicable law. An inquiry does not automatically subscribe you.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, these purposes can involve steps toward a contract, legitimate business interests, legal obligations or consent. You can withdraw consent for processing that relies on it.

04 / Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies and similar browser storage can support website functions and remember preferences. Their use depends on the features available on the website.

Where optional analytics or embedded services are used, they can collect information about website use. We provide information and choices required by applicable law.

You can manage cookies and stored website data through your browser settings. Blocking them can affect some features. Dismissing a notice is not consent to optional tracking.

05 / Sharing and service providers

We can share relevant information with providers that support hosting, communications, file storage, accounting and project delivery. Their access is limited to the relevant services.

Specialist collaborators and production partners can receive information needed for agreed work, subject to appropriate confidentiality and data protection obligations.

We do not sell personal information. We can share information at your direction or with your consent, including introductions to project partners.

We can also disclose information to meet a legal obligation, respond to valid legal requests or protect rights and safety. Any disclosure remains subject to applicable law and our contractual duties.

FREN is based in the United States. Communications and service providers can involve processing in the United States or other countries with different privacy laws. Applicable transfer requirements still apply.

Links to client sites, social networks and email services lead to services with their own privacy policies.

06 / Retention and security

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described here, including project delivery, contractual records and legal obligations.

The appropriate period depends on the record, our relationship and applicable requirements. We delete or anonymize information when it is no longer needed.

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information from unauthorized access, loss or misuse. Access is limited according to business needs.

No internet transmission or storage system is completely secure. Please use an agreed secure channel for sensitive project files. Do not send passwords, payment card details or medical records through general inquiries.

07 / Your choices and rights

You can contact us to request access, correction or deletion of your personal information. Depending on your location, you can also have rights to portability, restriction or objection.

Send requests to support@freninc.com with the subject “Privacy request.” Describe the information and action involved. Do not include sensitive identity documents in your first message.

We can request limited information to verify your identity or authority. Applicable law determines response periods, exceptions and any right to appeal or complain to a privacy regulator.

If you receive marketing emails from us, you can use the unsubscribe option or contact us to stop them. Necessary project and administrative messages can continue.

08 / Children and sensitive information

This portfolio and business inquiry website is intended for adults who commission or collaborate on design work. It is not a service for children.

We do not seek personal information from children under 13 through this website. If a child has provided information, contact us so we can investigate and address it.

Our healthcare and education projects can involve special requirements. Do not submit patient information, student records or identifiable research data without an agreed process and appropriate authorization.

09 / Changes and contact

We will update the date on this page when the policy changes. For material changes, we will provide further notice or obtain consent where required.
