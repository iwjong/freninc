Areas of Focus

Spatial design, storytelling, and creative technology across five fields. We bring each place's purpose to life, from concept to installation.

  • Education

    • Interactive exhibits
    • Learning through play
    • Digital storytelling
    • Character design
    • Hands-on discovery

    We turn complex ideas into experiences children and families can learn from by doing. Physical play, original characters, and digital stories invite exploration at every pace, making each discovery part of a larger story.

  • Culture & Civic

    • Exhibition design
    • Spatial storytelling
    • Wayfinding
    • Environmental graphics
    • Visitor journeys

    We turn cultural and community stories into places people want to explore. Local identity, original artwork, and interactive media come together in a clear visitor journey that invites people to pause, participate, and connect.

  • Parks & Recreation

    • Play environments
    • Experience planning
    • Interactive installations
    • Master planning
    • Interactive lighting

    We shape outdoor places for movement, discovery, and time together. From focused play areas to community destinations, we connect play structures, landscape stories, and interactive installations with space for active exploration and quiet rest.

  • Entertainment

    • Immersive media
    • Responsive environments
    • Animation & sound
    • Projection
    • Sensor-based interaction

    We create worlds visitors can influence through movement and play. Story, sound, moving images, and responsive technology work as one, giving each interaction a purpose and turning a room into a shared experience.

  • Healthcare

    • Care environments
    • Wayfinding systems
    • Interactive play
    • Healing gardens
    • Therapeutic playrooms

    We design around the daily realities of patients, families, and care teams. From pediatric wayfinding to therapeutic playrooms, original artwork and interactive media make room for orientation, comfort, and play throughout the hospital journey.
