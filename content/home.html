Our Purpose

We design with a clear intention: to make everyday environments more welcoming, understandable and engaging.

  • Start with people

    Listen to children, families and staff. Let their needs guide the experience.

  • Connect space and story

    Bring physical details, original artwork and digital media into one coherent environment.

  • Invite exploration

    Create opportunities to move, notice, learn and play in different ways.
